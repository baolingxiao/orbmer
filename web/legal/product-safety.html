Product Information and Safety Disclaimer

Effective: July 23, 2026Version: product-safety-2026-07-23Status: Draft

Orbmare does not claim a product is tested, certified, authorized, genuine, child-safe, food-safe, medical-grade, or suitable for a critical use unless verifiable documentation and the exact scope of the claim are displayed.

Supplier information

Descriptions, materials, dimensions, origin, images, warnings, and compatibility may be based partly on supplier-provided information. Unknown fields display “Details pending verification.” Customers should not infer missing specifications.

Images

Each listing should identify whether imagery is an actual product photo, supplier image, or rendering. Until verified, the image source is shown as pending verification. Color and scale may vary by display and photography.

Safety and intended use

Review all item-specific warnings and manufacturer instructions before use. Do not use a product for children, food contact, medical, protective, load-bearing, electrical, structural, transportation, or other safety-critical purposes unless that exact use is supported by verified documentation.

Incidents and recalls

Stop using a product that appears unsafe. Contact [SUPPORT_EMAIL] with the order number and details. Emergency or medical concerns should be directed to appropriate emergency or healthcare services. Orbmare may notify customers of a verified recall or safety issue when sufficient information is available.

Rights preserved

This disclaimer does not exclude product remedies, reporting duties, or other responsibilities that cannot be excluded under applicable law.
