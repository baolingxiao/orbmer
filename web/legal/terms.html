3. Orders and acceptance

Submitting payment or an offer is a request for Orbmare to review and source the item. It does not mean a third-party supplier has confirmed availability. An order is accepted only when Orbmare sends an explicit acceptance or purchasing confirmation. Orbmare may reject or cancel a request that is unavailable, restricted, incorrectly priced, unsafe, suspected of fraud, or not reasonably procurable. If payment has been captured, an applicable refund will be initiated.
