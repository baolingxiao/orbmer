## Brand idea

Orbmare / 傲马 should make global discovery feel considered, legible, and dependable. The brand is not built around limitless choice; it is built around helping people cross the gap between finding something distinctive and receiving it with informed expectations.

## Brand promise

The proposed brand promise is:

> Selected with intent. Purchased with clarity. Coordinated across borders.

This promise describes the intended experience and is not a legal guarantee of product quality, authenticity, delivery timing, or customs clearance.

## Brand principles

### Curated, not crowded

Show why a product belongs. Avoid treating catalog volume as proof of value.

### Global, not generic

Preserve useful context about origin, maker, material, category, and cultural relevance without exoticizing countries or reducing them to stereotypes.

### Premium through confidence

Premium presentation should come from trustworthy information, calm service, and resolved details—not decorative luxury claims.

### Human when exceptions occur

Cross-border commerce produces uncertainty. Status and support language should be specific, respectful, and action-oriented.

### Honest about boundaries

State when pricing is estimated, when duties may be collected separately, when a merchant controls availability, and when the Platform coordinates rather than directly performs a service.

## Voice

- Clear rather than legalistic.
- Calm rather than urgent.
- Informed rather than absolute.
- Helpful rather than paternalistic.
- Internationally respectful rather than culturally generic.

## Claims guardrails

Do not use claims such as `guaranteed authentic`, `all duties included`, `risk-free`, `official partner`, or `delivery guaranteed` unless the evidence, operating process, remedy, and legal approval exist.

> [LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED: LR-002] Review customer-facing brand promises, trust badges, authenticity claims, protection language, and comparative claims before launch.

## Brand architecture

Orbmare / 傲马 is currently treated as the umbrella brand. Regional pavilions and vertical stores may organize discovery beneath it, but their naming, endorsement, and legal disclosure rules are not final.

> [DECISION REQUIRED: DR-014] Approve the relationship among the umbrella brand, country/region pavilions, category stores, certified collections, and any partner brands.
