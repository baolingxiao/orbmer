## Positioning statement

For buyers who want distinctive products that are difficult to purchase reliably across borders, Orbmare / 傲马 is a curated purchasing and transaction-coordination Platform that combines product selection, understandable order economics, international milestone visibility, and one support entry point.

Unlike open listing marketplaces or forwarding-only tools, the proposed Platform emphasizes controlled assortment, role clarity, and coordinated resolution. This distinction remains a hypothesis until supported by customer and operational evidence.

## Short-term positioning

The MVP should be positioned as a controlled cross-border purchasing service:

- a narrow assortment or request scope;
- selected source merchants or purchasing partners;
- Platform-led ordering and support coordination;
- explicit description of the Legal Seller and agency relationship;
- visible fees and import assumptions; and
- no implication that the Platform directly controls every merchant, carrier, or customs event.

## Long-term positioning

The Platform may evolve toward a controlled Marketplace in which selected merchants or brands participate directly under consistent catalog, fulfillment, and support standards. It may selectively assume additional transaction responsibilities when doing so improves customer value and the legal/economic case is proven.

## Role boundaries

| Capability | Proposed MVP role | Boundary requiring approval |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery and curation | Platform-controlled | Criteria and prohibited claims |
| Ordering interface | Platform-controlled | Whether the order is an agency instruction, marketplace order, or direct sale |
| Payment presentation | Platform-coordinated | Payee, fund flow, receipt issuer, tax treatment, and refund control |
| Procurement | Platform or approved partner coordinates | When title or risk transfers |
| Fulfillment | Merchant, warehouse, or logistics partner performs; Platform tracks | Carrier contract, loss allocation, and customs representation |
| Customer support | Platform provides entry point and coordinates | Remedy authority and financial responsibility |
| Product assurance | Baseline information plus possible tiered protection | Verification standard, exclusions, and remedy |

## Positioning exclusions

The Platform should not position itself as:

- a universal importer for every buyer and destination;
- an unrestricted marketplace open to any seller;
- a guaranteed-delivery carrier;
- an insurer without an approved insurance structure;
- the manufacturer of third-party goods; or
- Merchant of Record unless the transaction and disclosures are explicitly structured that way.
