## One-sentence definition

Orbmare / 傲马 is a proposed global curated purchasing and cross-border transaction Platform that helps buyers discover and acquire selected products from merchants in other countries through a coordinated ordering, payment presentation, tracking, and support experience.

## The problem

Cross-border discovery is fragmented. A buyer may find a product but still face uncertainty about:

- whether the listing and merchant are trustworthy;
- whether the merchant sells to the buyer's destination;
- the expected total cost after service fees, shipping, duties, and taxes;
- who purchased, sold, shipped, imported, or supports the order;
- where the order is across merchant, warehouse, carrier, and customs handoffs; and
- what remedy is available when an order is unavailable, late, damaged, incorrect, or disputed.

Small merchants and brands face a complementary problem: international demand is difficult to serve without localized acquisition, support, payment, logistics, and compliance operations.

## Proposed product

The Platform is intended to combine:

1. curated cross-border product discovery;
2. a controlled request or ordering flow;
3. understandable price and fee presentation;
4. procurement coordination with merchants or purchasing partners;
5. international fulfillment and tracking coordination;
6. customer-support coordination across parties; and
7. tiered protection for eligible products and transactions.

## What the project is

- A cross-border commerce coordination service.
- A curated catalog and trust layer.
- An operating system connecting buyers, merchants, brands, sourcing partners, logistics providers, payment providers, and support.
- An asset-light business by design, generally avoiding long-term inventory ownership.
- A product whose legal and operational role may evolve in controlled stages.

## What the project is not

- It is not an unrestricted seller-listing marketplace at the MVP stage.
- It is not a promise that every product worldwide can be purchased or imported.
- It is not a freight carrier, customs authority, bank, card network, or insurer merely because it coordinates those services.
- It is not automatically the Legal Seller or Merchant of Record for every transaction.
- It is not an unlimited guarantee of third-party product quality, authenticity, delivery timing, or customs outcome.
- It is not a plan to build and hold broad speculative inventory.

## Participants and value

| Participant | Core need | Proposed value |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer | Access, clarity, trust, and help across borders | Curated discovery, unified ordering, visible milestones, cost explanation, and coordinated support |
| Source Merchant | Incremental international demand without building every cross-border function | Qualified demand and structured fulfillment instructions |
| Brand | Controlled discovery and credible presentation in new markets | Curated brand context, customer insights, and optional enhanced verification |
| Sourcing Partner | Repeatable demand and standardized instructions | Operational tooling, clear service levels, and measurable partner performance |
| Logistics Partner | Accurate shipment data and predictable handoffs | Structured order information and exception coordination |
| Platform | Sustainable economics from coordination and trust | Service fees, commissions, logistics services, protection services, or membership, subject to approval |

## Scope boundaries

The transaction role is not yet final. The MVP direction is a controlled Proxy Purchasing Agent model, while a controlled Marketplace is a possible later stage. Full Merchant of Record responsibility is not recommended until the supporting legal, payment, tax, insurance, compliance, and operational systems are complete.

## MVP hypothesis

A narrow, manually controlled service can validate whether buyers will pay for trusted access and coordination before the Platform invests in marketplace self-service or assumes broader seller responsibility.

The MVP should constrain categories, source countries, destination countries, partners, and order volume. Its purpose is to prove demand, successful delivery, buyer trust, and contribution economics—not to maximize catalog size.

> [DECISION REQUIRED: DR-009] Define the initial buyer segment, category wedge, source countries, destination countries, and maximum pilot volume.

> [DECISION REQUIRED: DR-008] Define prohibited, restricted, and unsupported products and destinations before catalog acceptance begins.

## Known limitations at Foundation stage

- No final contracting entity or launch jurisdiction is specified.
- No approved legal seller, payment collection, invoice, refund, chargeback, or importer allocation exists.
- No approved fee schedule, protection promise, category policy, or service-level objective exists.
- Product and operating documents for phases `02` through `12` have not yet been written.
- Existing software behavior must not be interpreted as approved commercial policy.
