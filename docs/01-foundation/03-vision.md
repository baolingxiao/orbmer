## Vision statement

Become a trusted gateway through which buyers can confidently access distinctive products across borders and merchants can enter selected international markets without independently rebuilding the full commerce stack.

## Long-term product state

If the model is validated, the Platform could provide:

- region and category discovery built around curation rather than open listing volume;
- controlled seller or sourcing-partner participation;
- consistent catalog, price, tracking, and support standards;
- route-specific logistics and customs guidance;
- evidence-based authenticity and product-protection tiers;
- data-informed selection for merchants and brands; and
- selective expansion of transaction responsibility where the economics and legal architecture justify it.

This is a direction, not a promise that the Platform will operate every capability or assume the same legal role in every market.

## Strategic horizon

### Near term

Validate a narrow Proxy Purchasing Agent-style service through manually controlled cross-border lanes, curated products, and measurable service standards.

### Medium term

Develop a controlled Marketplace for selected merchants and brands after onboarding, enforcement, support, and transaction controls are proven.

### Long term

Operate a portfolio of transaction models by category and jurisdiction, potentially including selective Merchant of Record arrangements only where infrastructure and risk capacity are sufficient.

## Vision constraints

- Trust must grow faster than catalog breadth.
- Role clarity must grow with transaction complexity.
- Operational evidence must precede automation.
- Expansion must be lane-specific, not assumed globally.
- The Platform should remain asset-light unless inventory ownership has a documented strategic and economic reason.
