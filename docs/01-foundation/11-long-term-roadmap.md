## Roadmap principle

Progression is evidence-gated, not date-guaranteed. A stage begins only when its legal, operational, product, technical, and economic entry conditions are met.

## Stage 1 — Controlled purchasing MVP

### Objective

Prove that a narrow Proxy Purchasing Agent-style service can create trusted access and complete cross-border orders with understandable economics.

### Scope

- one primary buyer segment;
- a narrow category wedge;
- limited source and destination countries;
- approved Source Merchants or purchasing partners;
- manually controlled catalog and order acceptance;
- unified ordering, payment presentation, tracking, and support coordination;
- explicit importer, fee, estimate, and protection disclosures; and
- no broad seller self-service.

### Required deliverables

- approved transaction and contracting structure;
- category and destination eligibility matrix;
- order and exception responsibility matrix;
- fee and refund rules;
- partner service levels;
- buyer-facing disclosures and legal drafts;
- auditable order ledger and milestone taxonomy; and
- pilot dashboard using the success metric framework.

### Exit evidence

- repeatable sourcing and delivery performance;
- acceptable material-claim and chargeback levels;
- positive contribution margin for the target lane or a documented path to it;
- evidence of repeat demand or strong referral;
- documented resolution for major failure types; and
- no unresolved launch-blocking legal or safety issue.

## Stage 2 — Controlled Marketplace

### Objective

Allow selected merchants or brands to participate more directly while preserving curation, service standards, and buyer trust.

### Candidate capabilities

- structured merchant onboarding and verification;
- merchant catalog submission with Platform approval;
- inventory or availability synchronization;
- seller service levels and enforcement;
- payment settlement and reserves;
- standardized returns and disputes;
- managed logistics options;
- enhanced product verification; and
- partner performance scoring.

### Entry gates

- Stage 1 metrics meet approved thresholds;
- seller agreement and marketplace disclosures are legally approved;
- seller identity, restricted-product, sanctions, tax, and payout controls are operational;
- refund and chargeback allocation is enforceable;
- support can handle multi-seller exceptions; and
- the controlled Marketplace improves selection or economics without degrading trust.

> [DECISION REQUIRED: DR-012] Approve seller admission, control level, settlement, reserves, enforcement, and the minimum evidence required to enter Stage 2.

> [LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED: LR-010] Review marketplace operator duties, seller verification, platform liability, payments/payouts, tax reporting, consumer protection, product safety, and notice/takedown obligations.

## Stage 3 — Selective transaction ownership and scaled network

### Objective

Expand supported lanes and selectively assume Merchant of Record or managed logistics responsibility where it produces superior customer value and sustainable risk-adjusted economics.

### Candidate capabilities

- transaction-model routing by jurisdiction, product, seller, and destination;
- selective MoR entities or providers;
- landed-cost calculation and duties-paid shipping where supported;
- multi-currency pricing and settlement;
- regional returns or consolidation hubs;
- category-specific authenticity and safety programs;
- merchant and buyer membership services; and
- deeper brand and logistics integrations.

### Entry gates

- tax, payment, invoicing, consumer-law, insurance, privacy, sanctions, and product-liability architecture is approved;
- sufficient reserves, supplier recourse, and working capital exist;
- each lane has an approved importer and customs model;
- MoR margin exceeds the incremental operational and risk cost;
- recalls, fraud, disputes, and returns can be managed at the proposed volume; and
- governance can suspend a category, partner, or lane quickly.

> [DECISION REQUIRED: DR-015] Approve MoR eligibility by transaction class and jurisdiction; the Platform must not default globally to MoR.

> [LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED: LR-011] Conduct jurisdiction-specific review before the Platform or a contracted provider acts as MoR, importer, distributor, or seller.

## Cross-stage workstreams

- Product and catalog governance
- Buyer protection and dispute operations
- Merchant and partner quality
- Logistics and customs
- Payments, tax, finance, and treasury
- Trust, safety, and regulatory compliance
- Data, security, privacy, and observability
- Brand, localization, and customer support

## Stop or narrow conditions

A lane, category, partner, or stage should be paused when:

- legality or safety cannot be established;
- role or cost disclosures are materially misleading;
- exception rates exceed approved thresholds;
- payment access or fraud exposure is threatened;
- contribution economics remain structurally negative;
- partner performance cannot be corrected; or
- support capacity cannot provide the promised remedy.
